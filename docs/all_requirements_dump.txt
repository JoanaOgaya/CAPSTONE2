Integrations with STARS and DMS
The six integrations from your integration document stay, rewritten against the new
statuses and rules. Three rules apply to all of them: every message is keyed by an
idempotency key (waybill number, invoice number or request ID) so a retry never creates
a duplicate; failed calls are retried and logged; and no integration can set an invoice to
Paid. Only the Finance check in FOMS can do that.
STARS and FOMS
1. Invoice endorsement and client acknowledgment
Trigger and direction: Invoice becomes Approved and is released. FOMS → STARS →
FOMS
Data exchanged: Out: invoice number, client details, invoice document, remarks. Back:
STARS task ID, assignee, signed receiving copy, received date, completion result
FOMS handling: Status moves Sent, then Received. The due date is set to received
date + 30 days. The receivable already exists from Sent (ARC-01, ARC-02). No return in
about 7 days triggers a reminder (ARC-05)
Changed from Capstone 1: Receivable is recorded at Sent, before the due date is
known. Status names align with BIL-09
